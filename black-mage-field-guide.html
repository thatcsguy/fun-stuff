01 / Mental model

One engine. Charge it. Empty it.

Black Mage is a resource cycle disguised as a long spell list. Ice sets up the next fire phase; fire converts that setup into damage.

The setup phase

Umbral Ice

Your damage is quieter here because this phase is buying the resources that make Astral Fire efficient.

  • 1Refill MP. Ice spells in Umbral Ice restore your fuel.
  • 2Cast Blizzard IV. Three Umbral Hearts discount your first three Fire IV casts.
  • 3Use ice Paradox. It is strong, instant, free here, and gives you a movement or weave window.
The damage phase

Astral Fire

Fire spells hit harder, cost more, and natural MP recovery stops. You are deliberately spending everything.

  • 1Cast six Fire IVs. They are your repeatable heavy hit and each builds one Astral Soul.
  • 2Use fire Paradox. It deals damage and guarantees the Firestarter proc for your next entry.
  • 3Cash out. Six Souls unlock Flare Star; Despair spends your final MP.

The central habit

Move during damage, not instead of it.

Bank instant casts for mechanics you know are coming. Xenoglossy is especially valuable because it is both your strongest spell and a full movement window.

Since Patch 7.2, most casts are shorter than their recast. Triplecast and Swiftcast are primarily mobility and weaving tools—not mandatory speed buffs.

Ley Lines rule: place them where you can use most of the 20 seconds. Stand near an edge, dodge across the circle, return with Between the Lines, or relocate the circle once with Retrace.
04 / Tools

Every cooldown answers a specific problem.

Open a card for the reasoning. If you know the problem a tool solves, you will know when to press it.

LL

Ley Lines

Use roughly on cooldown where the full circle can work.
Why: it shortens both cast and recast time by 15% for 20 seconds, so every GCD—including instant ones—benefits. It does not need to be saved specifically for fire.
MF

Manafont

Press at the tail of fire, after you have spent your MP.
Why: it fully restores MP and gives Astral Fire III, three Hearts, Thunderhead, and Paradox. Using it early wastes fuel; using it after the cash-out buys a complete second fire phase and Flare Star.
AMP

Amplifier

Use near cooldown—unless Polyglot is already capped.
Why: it grants one Polyglot. Delaying risks losing a use; pressing at three stacks throws the new stack away. Spend a Xenoglossy first.
TC

Triplecast / Swiftcast

Reserve them for planned movement and clean weaves.
Why: they no longer speed up most of the normal loop. Their value is preserving casts while your feet are moving. Weave them after an instant spell where possible.
HT

High Thunder

Refresh when the DoT has less than about 3 seconds.
Why: each element swap gives Thunderhead, but that does not mean each proc should be spent. Early refreshes cut off valuable ticks; late refreshes create downtime.
XENO

Xenoglossy

Spend for movement, raid buffs, or before the third stack overcaps.
Why: it is your strongest single-target spell and instant. Movement takes priority over perfect buff alignment; a missed Polyglot stack is damage you can never recover.
AM

Aetherial Manipulation

Teleport to someone who already solved the mechanic.
Why: it covers distance faster than running. Use it when a party member is safely positioned; keep your target selection method comfortable and reliable.
US

Umbral Soul

During downtime, rebuild ice, MP, and Hearts for free.
Why: when there is nothing to hit, enter Umbral Ice with Transpose if needed, then arrive at the next target fully prepared instead of starting empty.
MIT

Addle / Manaward

Plan Addle for raid damage; use Manaward before personal danger.
Why: dead casters do zero damage. Addle is strongest against magic damage; Manaward shields up to 30% of your maximum HP. Defensive planning is uptime planning.
Three or more targets

Freeze once. Flare twice.

The AoE loop is shorter and more explosive. Instant filler spells create safe weave windows for Transpose.

Freeze→Foul / HT II+Transpose→Flare ×2→Flare Star→Foul / HT II+Transpose

Why this works: Freeze in Umbral Ice I gives enough MP and three Hearts for two Flares. Each Flare gives three Astral Souls, so two unlock Flare Star. Transpose skips the weaker High Fire II / High Blizzard II swaps.

Manafont: after the first Flare Star, it funds two more Flares and a second Flare Star. On exactly two targets, use this AoE shape but replace Freeze with Blizzard IV.

Your mantra

Always know your next safe cast.

Damage comes from uninterrupted GCDs. The rotation tells you what is valuable; planning tells you whether you can finish it.

03
Am I approaching Polyglot cap?Spend Xenoglossy before a stack is lost.
