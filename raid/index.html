HOW TO PLAY

MOVE & FIGHT

Left thumb anywhere (or WASD) to move. Attacks are automatic: blade up close, glass needles at range. Abilities never miss.

READ THE FLOOR

Red: get out. Gold: stand in. Violet: special rule.

STEP & RECALL

Phase Step through a hit as it lands for SLIPSTREAM (the charge comes back). It leaves an Echo: Recall rewinds you to it, untouchable on the way, and cleanses Entropy.

FRACTURE & SHATTER

Rend cracks the boss (5 max). Shatter cashes them in and heals you for each one (the green on your HP bar). 5 = GRAND SHATTER. Fill the Hourglass for ZERO HOUR.

STASIS LANCE

Violet cast bar? Lance it to INTERRUPT (short cooldown). No violet cast? It PIERCES: a heavy hit, +2 Fractures, boss takes +20% for 5 s, but a long cooldown.

RINGS

Shock rings can't be outrun. Each has a gap: stand in the green lane. Or Phase Step when the circle at your feet closes and ticks.
