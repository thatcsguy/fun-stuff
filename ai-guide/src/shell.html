AI Field Guide

How models, agents and the harnesses around them actually work. Written for an engineer who uses them every day.

How to read this

Eleven groups, each split into short topics that stand on their own. Read in order for a narrative from tokens to agents, or open the contents (☰) and jump to whatever you are curious about. Each group ends with a link to the next one, and the chips under a group title let you skip within it.

Depth is deliberately medium: enough mechanism to reason about what you see when you run an agent, not enough to implement one from a paper. Numbers that go stale (prices, context sizes, benchmark scores) are left out on purpose.
